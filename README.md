# 🍽️ Zomato Restaurant Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing Zomato restaurant data to understand restaurant performance, customer preferences, pricing trends, ratings, and service availability.  
The analysis is performed using **Excel, SQL, Power BI, and Tableau** to generate meaningful business insights and interactive dashboards.

The main objective is to identify popular cuisines, top-performing locations, restaurant ratings, cost patterns, and customer service trends such as online delivery and table booking.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze restaurant distribution across countries and cities
- Understand customer preferences based on cuisines and ratings
- Study price range and average cost trends
- Analyze online delivery and table booking availability
- Identify factors affecting restaurant performance
- Create dashboards for better business decision-making

---

## 📂 Dataset Information

**Dataset Name:** Zomato Restaurant Dataset

**Total Restaurants:** 9,551+  
**Countries Covered:** 15  
**Cities Covered:** 141  
**Cuisines Available:** 1,826  
**Average Restaurant Rating:** 2.89

## 📊 Key Analysis Performed

### 🍜 Cuisine Analysis
- North Indian cuisine has the highest number of restaurants
- North Indian + Chinese is among the most popular combinations
- Chinese and Fast Food cuisines also have high availability

### 🚚 Online Delivery Analysis
- Around 22.57% restaurants provide online delivery
- Most restaurants currently do not provide delivery services

### 📅 Table Booking Analysis
- Around 13.64% restaurants provide table booking facilities
- Majority of restaurants operate without table booking

### 💰 Cost Analysis
- Restaurants with higher average cost categories have significant presence
- Price segmentation helps understand customer spending patterns

### ⭐ Rating Analysis
- Many restaurants have low ratings
- Restaurants with excellent ratings are comparatively fewer
- Rating trends help identify customer satisfaction levels
